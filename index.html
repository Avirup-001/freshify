FREQUENTLY ASKED

Are your bowls vegan and gluten-free?
Yes! All of our bases are 100% vegan, made with almond or coconut milk. Most of our toppings are gluten-free, but please let us know about any severe allergies.
Do you use added sugars?
Never. The sweetness in our bowls comes entirely from real, organic fruits and a touch of raw honey or agave if requested.
Can I customize my bowl?
Absolutely! You can mix and match bases, remove ingredients, or load up on extra superfood toppings.
Do you deliver?
Yes, we deliver within a 5-mile radius through our delivery partners.
